GOOD TO KNOW

A few little answers.

Before you settle in, here are the essentials.

Is this a real restaurant?

Saffron & Ember is currently a sample restaurant project. This version stores order requests, table requests and contact messages on the computer running the Node server. It is not connected to a live restaurant.

How do reservations work?

Choose a date, party size and available time. The server checks opening hours, closed dates and seat capacity throughout your visit. Your request starts as pending. Staff must confirm it in the management dashboard. Check My requests for updates.

How do I pay?

The configured method is payment on collection or delivery. No online payment or card processing is connected. Staff can record payment received in the management dashboard.

Where do you deliver?

The checkout lists the currently configured delivery areas and fees. There may be a minimum delivery subtotal. These are sample service settings; confirm actual delivery arrangements with the real restaurant before launch.

Do you cater for dietary requirements?

Menu cards show dietary guidance and declared allergens. These labels do not guarantee an allergen-free kitchen. Include your requirements in the notes and discuss serious allergies with restaurant staff before ordering.

Can I cancel an order or reservation?

Use My requests with your private key. Orders can be cancelled while pending. Future pending or confirmed reservations can be cancelled before their arrival time. Once an order is being prepared or a visit has started, contact the restaurant.

Will I get an email or SMS?

Notifications are not connected. Save the reference and private key displayed after submission and check My requests. The website also remembers up to 20 references in this browser.

Can I arrange an event or a large group?

Use the contact form and select Private dining & events. Your message is saved in the management inbox. Group limits, hours and seat capacity can be configured by the administrator.

YOUR DETAILS, EXPLAINED

Privacy & data.

How this local website handles information.

What is stored

Order and reservation requests store the contact details you submit, request notes, relevant items or visit details, and status history in a local SQLite database. Contact messages store your name, email, subject and message. The restaurant administrator can access these records after signing in.

In your browser

Your shopping bag contains dish IDs and quantities in localStorage. My requests stores references and private access keys for up to 20 requests. Use Clear saved keys to remove those browser keys. Removing keys does not delete database records.

Security and access

Administrator passwords are hashed. The dashboard uses expiring HTTP-only session cookies. Private request keys are required to look up or cancel guest requests. Keep those keys private. Public tracking responses do not include contact details.

External services

This installation does not send email, SMS or online payments. Images are served locally. Typography is served from local font files. The external map link opens OpenStreetMap when you choose it.

Managing stored information

Records are retained on the server until the operator removes them using their database maintenance procedures. Administrators can export records, and local backups contain the same personal information. For a real business, replace this explanation with the operator's reviewed privacy policy, contact details and retention arrangements before public launch.

BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

Ordering & visits.

What a saved request means in this installation.

Requests and confirmation

Submitting creates a pending request. It does not guarantee restaurant acceptance, delivery timing or a confirmed table. Staff update the status in management; you can check it under My requests. This sample business is not an operating restaurant.

Prices and payment

The server calculates prices from the current menu. Tax, when configured, is calculated on the item subtotal; delivery is added separately. Fees are displayed before submission. Payment is due on collection or delivery. Online payments are not connected.

Changes and cancellations

You can cancel pending orders and future pending or confirmed reservations using your reference and private key. Contact the operator for other changes. The dashboard can record payment and refund handling; it does not transfer or refund money through a provider.

Food and allergies

Photographs are illustrative. Dietary labels and allergen information must be verified by restaurant staff. Contact the restaurant before ordering if you have allergies or other dietary requirements.

Before a real launch

The operator must replace sample business details and publish their actual ordering, payment, delivery, refund, privacy and cancellation terms. These pages describe the local software's behaviour.
