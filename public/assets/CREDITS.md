# Image credits

The JPEG photographs are illustrative images from Unsplash. They depict a
restaurant concept and sample food, not an actual business or exact menu dishes.
Source identifiers and download sizes are recorded in `scripts/fetch-assets.mjs`.

Source image links use `https://images.unsplash.com/<photo-id>`.
Unsplash license: https://unsplash.com/license

The flame favicon is an original SVG created for this project.
Typography: DM Sans and Playfair Display, downloaded from Google Fonts and served
locally, with Arial/Georgia fallbacks. SIL Open Font License notices are included
as dmsans-OFL.txt and playfairdisplay-OFL.txt. Normal use needs no font service.
